# NU Sawangan homepage

## Scope and mode
Landing page for the existing public homepage route. Persuade.

## Audience and job
Confirmed audience: residents and the wider community who want to understand NU Sawangan's activities and read its news. Primary action: open recent news; secondary paths are the member directory and activity gallery.

## Content and proof
Use only the existing published-news collection, the real statistics passed to the homepage, existing public routes, and the NU mark already in public storage. No invented news, counts, testimonials, or contact details. The requested jamaah/pesantren photos are not present in the repository; use named local image slots with a composed fallback until the organization supplies licensed images.

## Constraints
Preserve all routes, controller behavior, database access, and existing data. Rebuild only the homepage presentation. Follow the user-pinned white/green palette, Plus Jakarta Sans or Poppins, spacious reference composition, semantic HTML, responsive layout, WCAG AA contrast, and reduced-motion preference. Use no external image hotlinks.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A contemporary NU Sawangan public bulletin that turns the community's real news and activity archive into a clear invitation to read and participate; it refuses the existing logo-tile hero and a wall of generic equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: White space is the paper field; NU forest green is the ink for actions and emphasis; Plus Jakarta Sans carries editorial hierarchy. Fine green rules, a single active navigation indicator, and a vivid documentary photo carry the structure without simulating a printed poster.

STORY: Visitors recognize NU Sawangan immediately, see a direct route into current news, then discover organizational information, members, and the activity gallery. Only real published stories and current database statistics are proof.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At wide desktop, transparent sticky navigation sits over a three-part hero: large two-weight headline and pill actions at left, a tall jamaah photograph at center, and one compact latest-activity card at right. Scroll cue anchors the left edge. At mobile, headline/actions precede the full-width image and compact information card.

FORM: Brief-pinned contemporary NU bulletin, candidate 6 of seven locally grounded communication and community forms; seed key c86eaeee. The roll's monochrome data-field challenger was declined because it contradicts the explicitly required green photographic identity and makes public news harder to read. Its discipline is retained as a real-data-only rule: statistics and article metadata must remain precise and legible.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
